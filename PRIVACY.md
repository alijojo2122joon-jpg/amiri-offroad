# Amiri Offroad — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

Amiri Offroad ("the game") is an off-road driving game for Android, made by an independent developer. This page explains what information the game handles.

## What the game stores on your phone

The game saves your progress (stages played, stars, coins, cars, colours and settings) **only on your own device**. It is not sent to the developer or to any server of ours. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online play. It does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Google Play version of the game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

- the device's advertising ID,
- the IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, language),
- how you interact with ads.

This is done by Google, not by the developer, under Google's own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit ad personalisation at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Watching an ad is always optional, except for a short ad that may appear between stages now and then.

## Children

The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the e-mail address shown on the game's Google Play page.
